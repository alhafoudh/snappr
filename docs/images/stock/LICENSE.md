with two restrictions: the images cannot be sold without significant
modification, and they may not be compiled to replicate a similar or competing
service. Each photo's own page said "Free to use under the Unsplash License" on
the date below; none is Unsplash+.

They were picked to have no identifiable faces, and downscaled to 2400px on the
long side (`vips thumbnail`, Q 90, ICC kept). Unsplash strips EXIF from its
downloads, so they carry none.

`cargo xtask screenshot` feeds them to the app in filename order. The app selects
only the first arrival of a session, so `01` is the photo on the stage of
`docs/images/app.png` and the one the README's demo images are made from.
